ABOUT TGPU LEGACY

Honouring Knowledge.
Nurturing Generations.

TGPU Legacy is dedicated to preserving the legacy of Tok Guru Pulau Ubi through authentic Islamic learning, strong adab and a practical, compassionate approach to education.

Our focus includes Quran, Tajwid, Iqra’, Hifz, Arabic and Islamic Education — delivered online so knowledge can continue to benefit families and future generations.
